#Mechanic
## The Ash Twin Project

The Ash Twin Project is one of the most important projects in Outer Wilds. It is a time-travel machine powered by the sun going supernova that sends [[Spacetime#Information|Information]] back in time 22 minutes as the final method of attempting to find the Eye.

Specifically, it sends three things:
1. A command for the cannon to fire at the beginning of the loop[^1]
2. The Probe Tracking Module's data through its statue / mask pair. This includes all probe trajectories and the coordinates of the Eye.
3. The stored memories from every active statue / mask pair.

The idea for the Ash Twin Project was given by Conoy in the Southern Observatory when they were trying to figure out how to send a probe in the right direction, after Pye and Ramie had discovered the Negative Time Interval.

The Ash Twin Project combines 3 separate technologies: The Negative Time Interval (requiring an Advanced Warp Core to handle the energy level), Gravity Crystals, and [[Nomai Technology#Information Manipulation|Information Manipulation]].

All of these things come from Brittle Hollow with its unique gravity, Black Hole Forge, and [[The Shard|Quantum Shard]]. The Advanced Warp Core was made at the Black Hole Forge by Poke, and the gravity crystals needed for the Gravity Cannon on the [[Nomai Technology#Orbital Probe Cannon|Orbital Probe Cannon]] was made at the Gravity Crystal Workshop.

[[The Shard]] is a strange case for the Ash Twin Project, as its unique behaviors were discovered on Ember Twin at the original [[Low-Light Observation Chamber]]. The information manipulation that the experiments in the LOC provided, along with the Negative Time Interval also discovered on Ember Twin in the High-Energy Lab, are what allowed Conoy to come up with the idea for the Ash Twin Project in the first place, as they were both prerequisites.

## Information Manipulation

As seen throughout the Outer Wilds star system, a few different technologies represent some form of instantaneous communication.[^2] This includes:

1. Projection stones
2. Projection pools
3. Statue / mask pairs

Unlike Warp Technology (Annona's Designs), it is never explained where these came from or how the Nomai found it.[^3]

Projection pools (and related text walls) use projection stones to communicate in both text and visually. The experiment from the original [[Low-Light Observation Chamber]] is what provided the means of information manipulation to the Nomai, or at least the fact that it could be separated from [[Spacetime#Matter|Matter]].

These technologies were later expanded to be built on Giant's Deep, as its thick atmosphere provided a naturally low-light environment (similar to the LOC) and a useful precaution while developing it. While Information does not intrinsically react to observation, the area came from quantum research, and the Nomai did not yet understand if every form of information reacted in a similar way.

This is also where the statues were later built at the Statue Workshop.

As stated in the [[Spacetime]] note, information is not canonically visible as a traveling stream of data. However, from a pure game-design perspective, and how Outer Wilds already represents information as seen at the end of the loop in [[Nomai Technology#The Ash Twin Project|the Ash Twin Project]], it will be shown as streams of moving Nomai text.

## Orbital Probe Cannon

While albeit a more simple technology compared to most others, it's one of the most vital components of [[Nomai Technology#The Ash Twin Project|the Ash Twin Project]]. The Gravity Cannon attached to it (similar to the ones found on Brittle Hollow and Ember Twin) uses gravity crystals from the Gravity Crystal Workshop to fire things at a high speed.

When the Orbital Probe Cannon receives the command from the Ash Twin Project to fire, it rotates in a random direction and fires the Probe at a high velocity.[^4] This intense power throughout the cannon, however, causes it to break at the start of every loop due to Avens and Mallow.[^5]

It is unknown how the Probe and the Ash Twin Project communicate with the cannon. The statue / mask pairs might be bidirectional in form of communication, though that does not explain how the Probe works unless:
1. A statue can connect to multiple masks
2. The Probe actually has a mask inside
3. The communication is bidirectional

And since Information can travel separately from Matter throughout spacetime, I figure some form of data transfer was made for long-distance.
