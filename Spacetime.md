There are two different types of objects that can travel through this medium.

## Information
Information is a bit of an abstract concept compared to matter. However, this is what the Nomai discover at the [[Low-Light Observation Chamber]]

Pure information in the game, during the loop cutscene, is visualized as [[Memory Flashback.gif|travelling Nomai text]] in the direction of travel[^5]. This, of course, is just a representation. It is hard to describe the concept of pure information. Keeping the visual representation of it through Nomai text is necessary, though.

After discovering the strange properties of [[The Shard|the Shard]] and how it seemingly traps pure information inside of its interior, the Nomai find how to control this seemingly fundamental property through their black-hole / white-hole pairs.

Pure information is not found traveling naturally within spacetime, with the exception of [[Quantum Behavior|quantum material]] and it's state of observation (or more accurately, information) affecting the object. ^e978e3
## Matter
Matter is the more familiar version of travel through spacetime. This is what is commonly shown in game.

- The black-hole on Brittle Hollow, connecting to the white-hole at the White Hole Station.
- The black-hole / white hole pair in the High Energy Lab
- The black-hole formed in the Vessel with the Advanced Warp Core.
- The warp technology found on the scout launcher.

This may also be related to how objects that travel into the vortex of the Eye [[Persistence|persist]] across into the next universal cycle after the observer goes into the Eye, rather than the information that travels from the conscious observer to affect the new cycle (see [[Persistence#^6b82c1|Persistence #5]]).[^2]

Information and Matter normally interact bidirectionally, in the sense that neither controls/holds the other as a substrate. Instead, it's what produces a coherent application of universal behavior / physics, such as visuals or simple behavior.[^6]
