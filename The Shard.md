#Location #Major #Phenomenon
**The Shard is one of the most important objects located inside of The Vestige.**

The Shard is an unusual quantum fragment whose properties and history form the basis of much of the mod's story.

---

The Shard is visually quite different from other quantum shards.

![[Shard vs Normal.png|797]]
> Left Shard being the full modded one, the right one being a normal quantum shard. 
> The effect on The Shard will obviously need some adjusting, but I think it's good for now.

While the shard behaves like any other quantum shard (it still originated from The Eye), it looks and behaves differently to others due to the way light and sound behaved in its origin universe[^1]. While it wasn't too different, it has a noticeable effect.

Regarding the rainbow colors and the sequence that appears embedded inside when moving around it, this is due to the unique effects that [[Quantum Behavior|quantum shards]] have on [[Spacetime#Information|Information]]. Because of this, it recorded its previous universes last moments (similar to what is occurring in the current universe) into its material with minor interference patterns. This is only visible when existing light is passed through it.

## History


### Nomai Transport
One of the first things the hatchling encounters in the mod (led to by the [[Museum Placard]]) is a dead Nomai who was transporting a fragment of the shard to the Southern Observatory on Brittle Hollow for further research after the Sun Station had failed to power the Ash Twin Project, trying to look for anything else that might help.

The Interloper arrived and erupted before they could finish transporting it.

[^1]: While quantum fragments technically have an origin from the Eye itself rather than its current universe, the amount of time it spent there made it practically the same as if it were its origin universe.
