The Shard is an unusual quantum fragment whose properties and history form the basis of much of the mod's story.

The Shard is a quantum fragment that, like the rest of the quantum shards, got flung off the quantum moon when it was first forming. Unlike most quantum material, however, it contains properties [[Persistence#Law of Assimilation|assimilated]] from a previous universal cycle.

These properties include its unique rainbow visuals (refer to the [[Previous Universe]]), but more importantly, the way in which it handles exposure to a sequence of events with a corresponding viewing angle. The interference patterns found inside of the Shard is that of the previous universe's death, with the stars exploding.

Much of its behavior is also contributed to by the fact that the rules of the current universe and those of the previous universe are interfering and causing strange visuals and behavior. This can happen with many rules that are mutually exclusive, though quantum material mitigates this with it's "pocket-universe" behavior (see [[Persistence#Quantum Objects (A Special Case)|Quantum Objects (A Special Case)]])

## History
The history of the Shard is mostly what makes it unique compared to the other shards from the Eye of the Universe and its moon.

As all quantum fragments, the Shard originated from the Eye. It contains the normal rules found within [[Quantum Behavior]]. The Previous Cycle also contained its own Quantum Moon, which flung out quantum material during its formation.

Many fragments got dispersed through the nearby Solar System, and within this solar system contained a Desert planet (similar to that of Ash Twin). This is where some of the fragments landed, and assimilated in over billions and billions of years.

This assimilation of the previous universe's laws is what caused the Shard to look and behave the way it does. While Quantum Shards do naturally reveal the separation of Information and Matter, this shard coincidentally behaves in a way that reveals that more visibly to the Nomai.

Near the end of this universal cycle, the way quantum material interacts with information and the laws of the new universe allows it to "record" the death of this universe into an interference pattern found within the material that changes with viewing angle. The Shard then finds its way back into the eye to then persist into the next universe, where it becomes a part of the Quantum Moon found in the Hearthians' solar system. Many other shards also assimilated this same behavior, but the Shard was alone in the fact that it managed to apply the [[Persistence#Law of Persistence|Law of Persistence]].

Similarly to the previous universe, the quantum material also got flung off the moon during its formation. The shard found its way onto the surface of Brittle Hollow, where the Nomai later discovered it and compared it to the other quantum shard found on its surface (the one near the Tower of Quantum Knowledge in the present time).

When the Shard was first discovered, the Nomai were still separated from each other on Brittle Hollow and Ember Twin, so they had no way of communicating this discrepancy. Later, when both groups were able to reunite, they explained their findings and similar viewings of the Quantum Moon and their local Quantum Shards, including the oddity of this one specifically.

Later, the [[Low-Light Observation Chamber]] was built specifically to study the Shard and its anomalous properties, leading way to new technology being found, the Ash Twin Project later being proposed, and the beginnings of [[The Argument]]. The Tower of Quantum Knowledge was also built as a general consolidation of all quantum knowledge, including discussing the findings of the Shard from the [[Low-Light Observation Chamber]].

## After the Sun Station Failed
After the Sun Station failed, many Nomai collected within the [[Low-Light Observation Chamber#The Sixth Vertex|Sixth Vertex]] to discuss and try and find new paths to search for a solution to finding the Eye of the Universe. A few ideas were proposed:
1. Examine the new comet entering the Solar System to see what it might hold.
2. Search through the research found within the Sixth Vertex to see if the Shard could provide any other way of finding the Eye of the Universe.
3. Find an alternative way for powering the Ash Twin Project.
4. Bring the fragment back to the Southern Observatory on Ember Twin where the Ash Twin Project was originally proposed to see if any new ideas could be found.

All of these ideas were executed. Specifically, the last one can be found at the beginning of the mod. The dead Nomai carrying the fragment out of the Northern Glacier can be found discussing bringing the fragment to the Southern Observatory while another group of Nomai go explore the Interloper.

Of course, this Nomai is one of the first things found at the beginning of the mod. (see the [[Endings#Archaeological Ending|Archaeological Ending]])

### What does the Shard really mean?
This is mostly where the mod gets a bit intentionally abstract.

The Shard, along with [[The Argument]], are metaphors in which represent the core message found within the Vestige:

> The choices we make are shaped both by what we understand of the past and by what we hope to create in the future.

The Shard holds onto its past through the sequence of events found within its internal interference. Its past shapes the way the Nomai proceed with their time in the Solar System.

The Argument that the Shard creates is a more literal manifestation of this motif. Both sides, the Understand and Pursue sides, represent understanding the past and using your past to influence your future respectively. The Understand side wants to understands its past more, try and figure out why it is the way it is, while the Pursue side wants to use its existing knowledge to its advantage and move on to create its future.

This is the emotional beat of The Vestige.
