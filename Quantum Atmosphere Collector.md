#Location #Minor
The Quantum Atmosphere Collector is a large, Nomai space-station in orbit around the [[Quantum Behavior|Quantum]] Moon.

The QuAC, as a shortened name, does exactly what it's name suggests. It features a collector that reaches into the atmosphere of the quantum moon to collect material needed for the [[Nomai Technology#The Ash Twin Project|Ash Twin Project]]. More specifically, the statue / mask pairs required for both communication between various components of the project and the memory transfer for conscious observers connected to them.

The QuAC is considered a minor location as it serves as a pure explanation for the origin of the Statues needed for the Ash Twin Project, as well as an extra explanation for the implementation of information control within the project itself.

---
## Explanation

The Statues used within the Ash Twin Project, along with their material, do not have any source of origin within the base game. The Nomai never explain how they manufactured these statues apart from the fact that they "sculpted" them.[^1] The Hearthians also describe the material as a "stone," though their ignorance about a lot of Nomai technology (along with their makeshift manufacturing) makes this a bit of an unreliable source.[^2]

However, the Statues contains 2 specific aspects that are related to the behavior of quantum material.

1. The Statue reacts to consciousness. We know this from Quantum Behavior such that a quantum shard can choose any state when its current state is not observed by a conscious observer. While the statues themselves do not exhibit this superposition behavior[^3], how they react to a consciousness is odd for any material outside of quantum mechanics.
2. More importantly, the statues are super informationally dense.[^2] As described in the [[Spacetime]] note, quantum material is especially good for the manipulation of information, as its behavior makes it easier to separate matter from information, allowing for communication and high-information density.

The benefit of using quantum material for the statues is how easy it makes for both storing and exporting information, which the Statues require for both memories and communication between various aspects of the ATP (such as the OPC, the Probe, or the ATP itself).

The problem with building it out of quantum material, however, was the scarcity that the Nomai had. The shards scattered around the solar system were few and far between, and they did not want to use what little material they had to try and build the statues as the shards were vital for the [[Low-Light Observation Chamber#Expansion|LOC Expansion]] studies.

The only other source of quantum material they had was the Quantum Moon. However, building a quarry on the Quantum Moon was not viable as it was the only source they had for finding the Eye and they did not want to damage it. The surface was also far too unstable in its 6 quantum states to feasibly manufacture anything, let alone making it work technologically.

The atmosphere was an exception for the Quantum Moon, as its state (apart from the location) does not meaningfully change between its 6 locations[^4][^5]. This proves a valuable source for the Nomai, thus making them construct a collector for the atmosphere to later condense into a form of stone at the Statue Workshop.
