#Location #Major 

The Previous Universe was the universe prior to the one inhabited by the Hearthians. The Eye of the Universe establishes a universal cycle, where a Universe is created and later destroyed to form a new one. When not encompassed by the [[Persistence#Law of Persistence|Law of Persistence]], [[Spacetime#Matter|Matter]] changes its local laws of physics. Why this process occurs is unknown.

The only locations in which parts of the previous universe are shown are within [[Low-Light Observation Chamber#Expansion|the Expansion's]] planetarium, the death of the universe within [[The Shard]] itself, and the [[Endings#Eye Ending|Eye Ending]] when the [[Previous Universe#The Desert Planet (and its star system)|Desert Planet]] is shown (one of the greatest showcases of the previous universe).

## Connection to the Shard

[[The Shard]] was a quantum object that split off from the Quantum Moon of this universe and fell onto a planet within a nearby solar system, similar to what happened with the existing Quantum Shards throughout the Hearthian star system.

The "oil-slick" or iridescent behaviors within this universe [[Persistence#Law of Assimilation|assimilated]] with the Shard over a long period of time, producing the unique informational behaviors and visuals seen at in the fragment and later the entire Shard:

![[Shard vs Normal.png]]

While the underlying material and physical structure of the Shard did not change (materialistic laws were shared between these universes), its way of interacting with light did.

## The Desert Planet (and its star system)
The Desert Planet is the planet in the solar system close to the Eye of the Universe, similar to the Hearthian solar system. It is the planet in which [[The Shard]] landed on, specifically at its north pole.

This planet is characterized by its ever-present iridescent storm. While the iridescence and the visual phenomena found on the planet are not uncommon in this universe, the combination of its cold, desert surface and the storm above it is.

Like many planets within the Outer Wilds universe, its north and south poles contain unique effects. The North Pole is the Eye of the Storm, where a large [[Iridescent Cucumbernimbus Clouds.webp|cumulonimbus cloud]] swirls on the planets rotational axis. This is also where the Shard is situated, with broken down buildings around it from the civilization that once lived there.

The player arrives, at least in the [[Endings#Eye Ending|Eye Ending]], at the south pole.

The Desert Planet is not the only planet found within the star system.[^2]
