#Location #Major 
The Low-Light Observation Chamber (or the LOC for short) is one of the main buildings inside of the mod. It is located inside of **Ember Twin**. ^tr-ff3g7m8rc

The Nomai originally had built the LOC for the purpose of studying [[The Shard]] and it's anomalous properties. It was built before the High-Energy Lab or the Ash Twin Project were even thought of, as the Shard was one of the first things that the Nomai group on Brittle Hollow had found.

However, it was later [[Low-Light Observation Chamber#Expansion|expanded]] to merge the findings found in the High-Energy Lab.

The low-light part of the building was an important aspect in making sure that the Shard could be studied without interference. While the Nomai were not too familiar with controlling light, they did make sure to take the precautions required.

Because of the required lack of light, the controls are located *outside* of the actual building, along with a projection pool with a stone that can only be found once the hatchling reaches the Expansion. The pool was originally how the Nomai checked the results without introducing any extra light getting in from entrances.[^1]
## Structure
The LOC is built around the number **6**. Because of this, it is built in a hexagonal format.

This is both a reference to the [[Quantum Behavior|Quantum Moon's]] six locations, and where each side contributes to the energy needed to power the [[Low-Light Observation Chamber#Final Experiment|Final Experiment]].

I'm unsure if the Nomai did this on purpose or not yet, but they did also choose this design for the power requirements.
## Experiment 1
Experiment 1 is the first room in which the hatchling arrives to when entering the LOC, at side **1**. At first, it is a completely pitch black room (unless the hatchling uses their flashlight). When turning on the lights from the external controls, you will find a few things.2
When you first walk in, you will see the experiment. There is a slot holder for [[The Shard]] (and the full thing is left here) that is rotatable via the external controls, a focused light source, and photosensitive paper.

When the experiment is activated, the light shines through the shard and onto the photosensitive paper. You repeat this a few times to record each angle of the Shard's interference onto the paper. There is then a slot where you can insert your scout to see each paper and the sequence that the shard is meant to show.

However, it is hard to make out what it actually is, leaving room for the [[Low-Light Observation Chamber#Observatory|Observatory]] later in the building.
