#Location #Major 
The Low-Light Observation Chamber (or the LOC for short) is one of the main buildings inside of the mod. It is located inside of Ember Twin. ^tr-ff3g7m8rc

The Nomai originally had built the LOC for the purpose of studying [[The Shard]] and it's anomalous properties. It was built after the reunion of the Ember Twin and Brittle Hollow groups with the goal of figuring out why the shard behaves the way it does.

However, it was later [[Low-Light Observation Chamber#Expansion|expanded]] to merge the findings found in the High-Energy Lab.

The low-light part of the building was an important aspect in making sure that the Shard could be studied without interference. While the Nomai were not too familiar with controlling light, they did make sure to take the precautions required.

Because of the required lack of light, the controls are located *outside* of the actual building.
## Original LOC
The original Low-Light Observation Chamber was a small research facility located just under the surface of Ember Twin.

This building contains the earliest research regarding the Shard. It's main focus was a small experiment:

The Shard (or at least a fragment of it) was placed into a rotating apparatus. This room contained a photosensitive piece of paper and a light. The light was focused and shined through the Shard, extracting part of its trapped information (the Nomai's first discovery of this phenomenon) onto the piece of paper.

The controls located outside of the building were made for this purpose. They controlled the lights of the building (and the Expansion, though unnecessary). The controls being located inside of the building could introduce unwanted external light sources.

This projected a picture of the scene located inside of the Shard onto the piece of paper. They repeated this experiment at regular intervals of rotation to get a full temporal view of what was happening inside. This contained two discoveries.

1. The discovery of separating pure information from matter.
2. The strange sequence of events located inside of the Shard.

They never figured out the second one. However, the first one is the one they really focused on.

After this experiment was conducted and the findings were located, they shut down the LOC as it had no use for them as a quantum research facility anymore. Most of its findings were presented both in the LOC itself and Tower of Quantum Knowledge on Brittle Hollow.

The player is able to use their probe in the recording apparatus (where the sheets of paper go) to see the sequence of events for themselves. However, it is most likely not clear as to what's happening still. This leads way to the [[Low-Light Observation Chamber#Observatory|Observatory]] in the Expansion.

The studies that occurred within the Tower of Quantum Knowledge and the original Low-Light Observation Chamber gave way to many of the technologies we can find throughout the solar system. This includes:
1. Projection stones
2. Projection pools
3. Memory statues and masks
4. The Ash Twin Project
### The Ash Twin Project Proposal
When the Negative Time Interval was discovered in the High-Energy Lab, a proposal was made in the Southern Observatory by a Nomai named Conoy.

>  CONOY: I believe I have a solution for that problem! Have you spoken with Ramie and Pye about the technology they’re developing?

Nomai designated to the original Low-Light Observation Chamber have not been decided yet, but it is most likely one of the Nomai questioning the Quantum Moon earlier in the timeline.

The Negative Time Interval introduced the temporal aspect needed for the idea of the Ash Twin Project to be proposed. The ability to send information back in time. They had the informational side with the technology developed from the discoveries of the experiment in the original LOC, and the Negative Time Interval provided a way to send that information back in time.

After this proposal was decided upon, they started to build an [[Low-Light Observation Chamber#Expansion|Expansion]] for the Low-Light Observation Chamber, and thus, the Final Experiment.

This was mostly the start of [[The Argument]] and where many Nomai split between pursuing the continuation of using this Information technology and wanting to conduct more experiments with the Shard to try and understand its properties better.
## Expansion
The Expansion is a hexagonal megastructure located within the center of Ember Twin.

Despite the name, the Expansion of the Low-Light Observation Chamber is hardly connected to the original building except for its entrance and purpose.

The Expansion's infrastructure was built around the number 6, in reference to the [[Quantum Behavior|Quantum Moon's]] 6 locations and to the Vessel's warp coordinate input. The Nomai did not intentionally build it around these concepts, but instead for the power requirements needed for the [[Low-Light Observation Chamber#The Final Experiment|Final Experiment]].

It was also later used as a central hub for the [[Nomai Technology#The Ash Twin Project|Ash Twin Project]].

That leaves 1 extra vertex for the hexagonal structure of the LOC, making 7 vertices total.

### Entrance to the Expansion
The entrance to the Expansion had a few requirements to be decided before the Nomai were able to construct the 6 radial corridors from the central Vertex.

To enter, they decided to build a Quantum Entangled Hallway:

This hallway has two parts. One is located within the Expansion, and one is located within the original Low-Light Observation Chamber. Both of these hallways end in a dead-end. These hallways swap places when unobserved.

To enter the Expansion, you turn off the lights for the original LOC and enter the hallway. You then toggle your flashlight off so your environment is unobserved. This entangles you with the hallway. When you turn it back on, you are now in the Expansion.

The reason they did this instead of a simple entrance is:
1. The Expansion requires perfect light-level control. While the Final Experiment itself did not require the lights to be off, the Nomai still were unsure of the effect that light had on information travel. This was simply a safety precaution.
2. The original Low-Light Observation Chamber was a Shard testing facility first, a quantum research facility second. They built this as another test for their understanding of quantum mechanics.
3. The Nomai enjoy fun and unconventional methods.

### The Final Experiment
Ironically, the Final Experiment was actually one of the first experiments conducted within the Expansion.

The central vertex of the Expansion was built after the proposal of the Ash Twin Project. It was built to test the combination of the two technologies needed for the idea to work:
1. Control information through [[Spacetime]] (found from the studies at the Low-Light Observation Chamber)
2. Send things located within the medium of Spacetime back in time (found from the findings at the High-Energy Lab)

They didn't know if these two things worked together. So, they built an experiment located within the core of Ember Twin.

This experiment contains a room that surrounds a central chamber. Within this chamber, the Shard (or a fragment of it) has 6 locations for its quantum superposition, one for each vertex (though unrelated). 

They had a pair of warp cores located at the top and bottom of the central chamber in which information extracted from the Shard's current position would be sent back in time by 5 seconds.

The energy requirement for the High-Energy Lab required a solar panel that powered an entire city. They mentioned that the energy requirement for the Negative Time Interval grows exponentially. They needed far more solar panels for the Final Experiment than they needed for the High-Energy Lab experiments.

So, they placed 6 solar panels at regular angular intervals around Ember Twin, all leading into the central Vertex. These planted the seeds for the later construction of the 6 outer vertices.

The central chamber has a large circular window that can open and close, wrapping around the area as to make it observed and not observed. If the predicted position found within the computer lines up with the current position of the Shard after 5 seconds have passed, then the experiment works.[^1]

This also leads into the [[Localized Rupture]] if triggered by the player.

### Outer Vertices
After the Final Experiment succeeded, the Outer vertices were constructed as a central hub for communicating plans and discussing the Ash Twin Project as a whole.

Each of the Outer Vertices (apart from the Sixth Vertex) represented a planetary body that was useful for the Ash Twin Project. The 5 locations required for it were:
1. Timber Hearth (supernova-proof material)
2. Brittle Hollow (gravity crystals for the Orbital Probe Cannon)
3. Giant's Deep (construction of the Memory Statues and Orbital Probe Cannon)
4. Sun Station (power for the Ash Twin Project)
5. Hourglass Twins (central hub for the Ash Twin Project)

> My gratitude to those who noted my imprecise language! Yes, the Sun Station is not a planet. I believe this has been sufficiently clarified.

While the High-Energy Lab was clarified to be the central hub for designing the Ash Twin Project, all that can be found there are blueprints of the towers. I believe this was meant for designing the towers specifically.

The Expansion was instead used for coordination of each location and their corresponding resources.

#### The Sixth Vertex
The Sixth Vertex was the central hub of continued research for the Shard. This was the [[The Argument#Understand|Understand]] group's main research location and contained lots of knowledge about the Shard, where it came from, and how it behaves.

The name is also a reference to the Sixth Location of the Quantum Moon.
