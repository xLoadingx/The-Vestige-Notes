Ghost matter is quite intriguing to me.

Though I have figured out as to why this isn't the case a while ago, it used to be a thought that interested me for a while. Ghost Matter is a very strange thing in the Outer Wilds universe even though I don't think it carries much significance for the theory of spacetime, or anything. It's implied in the name, it's simply a different form of matter.

Though much like the quantum shards, it's very unique in the way that it behaves compared to other parts of the star system. And again much like the shards, it ultimately goes unexplained. Astral Codec is what solves this and I believe parallels very nicely with the Vestige as both story-wise and explanation for odd behavior... wise. It believes Ghost Matter came from a ship that's a remnant of a previous universe powered by 6 of these super dense cores of ghost matter, though one broke off and went into the comet, I believe, thus causing the whole tragedy we all know of.

Anyways, the thought I had about Ghost Matter during my first playthrough was: What would've happened if that one Nomai near the ATP warp tower would've made it?

I know now of course that Ghost Matter penetrates normal matter, which I assume also includes the core of the Ash Twin Project, so they would've died anyways. But it was a fun thought for the time, and the reason I figured that out was both the wiki and the fact that Ghost Matter can be found in the caves of Ember Twin.

Though it is still interesting to me as to the pose that the Nomai died in. They seemed to be reaching for the warp tower as if they knew what was going to happen, as Ghost Matter was an instantaneous death for all of them. Strange.
