I don’t really have a ton of time (around 20 minutes again) but I do wanna talk about this.

The Quantum Atmosphere Collector, or whatever I’ll eventually call it (the name still feels odd to me), is I think a really neat idea. I wanted to scrap it at first just based off the name because it feels so generic.

But when you think about it, almost everything in Outer Wilds is generically named. Either that or because I wrote it, it always felt off to me, though I’m unsure if I want to change it. I mean, the Orbital Probe Cannon is a cannon that shoots a probe in orbit around Giants Deep.

Brittle hollow is a planet that’s brittle and hollow, the Black Hole Forge is a forge above(?) a black hole, etc. Perhaps it is the actual visuals and the looks that come with such a place that make them feel so novel.

But anyways, what actually is the Quantum Atmosphere Collector? I believe it has quite a neat timeline and is one of those things that seem unrelated to the central story line but actually makes a bit of sense, which I really need in this mod at the moment!

The Nomai had found the Shard, built the LOC, and found that information can be separated from matter, as the whole mod starts off as. After the Negative time Interval was discovered, the ATP was proposed at the Southern Observatory.

Now the Nomai didn’t necessarily need statues to accomplish the ATP. Information manipulation is quite primitive once actually separated from its corresponding matter. However, in both my notes and as far as the Nomai know, quantum material is the only source of matter that makes this a viable solution for finding the Eye.

All they needed to do was send information back in time, which was simple after the Final Experiment was tested in the center of Ember Twin. However, how do you actually get that separated information to mean anything afterwards? It needs to be reintegrated into matter so normal machinery can use it, as information does not interact with matter on a macroscopic scale.[^1]

And their only idea was, again, to use quantum material, as it’s the only source of matter they know of that can easily store and export information from its material. So that’s when they decide to build the Statues, both to communicate between the various aspects of the Ash Twin Project (Orbital Probe Cannon, the ATP itself, the Probe, etc.) and to make Nomai aware of the loop. This part is technically not necessary (without precaution of failure) as to the Nomai, the Eye would be found on the first loop since they were unaware of all previous ones. This is where it gets into time travel weirdness that I do not have the time to delve into.

But anyways, there was a slight issue with building the statues out of quantum material. They didn’t have enough of it with the quantum shards around the solar system[^2] as they did not want to lower their already small supply. The only other source of quantum material they have is the Quantum Moon.

But the problem with the quantum moon is that it’s surface is unstable. It corresponds to the 6 orbital locations the quantum moon has, making it difficult (or impossible) to construct anything comprehensible, let alone technologically functioning.

So, they decide that the atmosphere of the quantum moon (which is quantum itself) is the only large-scale source of quantum material they have, in terms of how much they need for the statues.

This is where the Quantum Atmosphere Collector comes from, and yet despite this seemingly coherent timeline, I still feel a bit bad about it. I don’t know what it is that causes this.

This also gives me good opportunities to incorporate it within other various parts of the solar system, due to the Quantum Moon’s 6 locations. A good example of this is the Orbital Probe Cannon around Giants Deep.

I had written some fun dialogue I liked last night about the interaction between these two. Specifically, the Nomai that worked on the memory statues also worked on the QuAC (yes this is intentional), and coincidentally, Daz’s love worked on the Orbital Probe Cannon.

I find I really enjoy writing the Nomai’s type of dialogue. They’re very scientific in nature despite the way they show love towards each other, and I believe keeping those relationships (and personalities) between the Nomai are quite important to make it sound both realistic and, well, good to me. This is especially important because a lot of other Outer Wilds mods I played really struggle in this aspect, usually because they write in far too modern English.

I will paste that dialogue here when I arrive home.

I do worry that the QuAC will interfere with the eerie nature of the quantum moon. The Quantum part of the game is somewhat of a free DLC itself and is completely unnecessary to learn to beat the game. However, it does explore the reality of the Eye and explains some of its strange behaviors.

The Vestige is very focused on the quantum aspect, though I do want the QuAC to leave an open-ended question of its own. My mind goes to Giants Deep for whatever reason. I find that the planet is quite strange in relation to most of the other planets, particularly because of its electrical core and strong winds.

Of course, its based off of a gas planet like Jupiter, but it really isn’t. It has a water surface, islands, even a coral-like core. It simply has a thick atmosphere. When I first played, I contributed this to the suns heat circulating the atmosphere, though that is not strong enough to cause such winds.

How can it have an electrical core without any flowing metals? Where does the current come from? Why is there coral at the center? Why is the atmosphere so chaotic anyways?

There’s many topics I’d like to ask, specifically, rather than answer. Whether for a future mod or simply for people to speculate on, I’m unsure. But I do want The Vestige to a little bit more than just quantum behavior.

But again, I’d like to reiterate just how much I loved writing the dialogue for the QuAC, at least the form of it I wrote in a couple of minutes.

Another fun fact about the QuAC is that the collector that reaches into the atmosphere is actually stationary at the South Pole despite the collector orbiting around the quantum moon. I feel like its similar to non-Euclidean geometry, like Dark Bramble, rather than teleportation to the South Pole. Just a small detail I’d like to note.

I write these notes not to write down what is canon, but to simply explore my current idea of, well, my current ideas. I only write what is going into the mod, at least at the time, into the note that is named as such.

I really want more locations such as this. Perhaps not as large (in terms of story or physical size), but just more locations that are a consequence of the central story line. I have a few ideas in the back of my mind, so we’ll see where that leads us! Well, me…
