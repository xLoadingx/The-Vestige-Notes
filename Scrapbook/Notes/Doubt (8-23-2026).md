I... I've been having a bit of doubt recently, about the whole project.

Not that I believe it's doomed, of course not, that'd be very short-sighted for a long-term project such as this. It's just a recent development that's pressed a lot of doubt into my mind about my technical abilities, and whether I possess enough skill to build this whole thing.

Again, I'd like to reiterate that I don't truly believe anything I mention here, but it is simply a byproduct of, well, a lack of development rather.

I've been working on the ending for the Eye scene, which honestly in hindsight is a bit backwards considering I haven't built anything else. I figure that building the actual thing first would be important to make sure the Eye scene makes sense...

But anyways, specifically the "previous universe wormhole" thing. It was supposed to be one of the "[[Fabric of Spacetime.webp|connector]]" things, but larger and more... spectacular, I guess. As mentioned in the spacetime note, I found that these connectors looked eerily similar to those of wormhole representations.

So I figured, why not make a giant one that led the player to a kind of "previous universe" from which the shard originated? I still enjoy this idea, hence why I don't consider the desert scene[^1] a bad idea.

But the actual part that leads the player there, the giant colorful "wormhole" type thing that had a bunch of the others orbiting around it, a giant cool looking thingy... clearly I haven't thought this out a whole bunch. But despite that, I built it.

![[Scrapped - Previous Universe Wormhole.webp|436]]
> I have no idea why it's so low-quality. Most likely a pasting issue.

And... I hated it? Well, "hate" is a strong word. I didn't like it, at all. It didn't feel like anything.

You can see the issue, right? There's nothing special about it. Whether it's just a lack of sound or contrast or whatever the reason was, I didn't even feel as if it was worth trying in game. Maybe it is.

And for whatever reason, this specific setback caused a lot of doubt within my mind, still does somewhat. I'm not sure why, I've had plenty of scrapped ideas before, heck that's why I have a scrap book in the first place.

And not just doubt for this, but the entire project. Do I actually have the technical ability to do what I want to do? Is it just going to be Outer Wilds Story Mod #389? Will this ever make sense to someone other than me?

I know those are all not true, for the most part. Sure, I do not have the technical capabilities of the entire team at Mobius Digital, and I've known that from the start.

Personally, I think it's because I just don't really actually... *have* anything for the mod made yet. I have the shard scene which I still can't tell if I like or not (I do, at least very much so compared to this, I mean "finished"), and I have a prototype for the final experiment. But... that's it. And I've also been procrastinating on writing these notes, which only adds to the doubt I have of ever finishing this project.

I don't hate the project, no, not at all. I love this thing and the ideas I've managed to come up with it. Realistically, this is just a small setback. There's countless other possibilities I have for leading the player towards the desert scene.

And yet, this seems to be the only one my mind can see. I'm just... going to check on what I've actually made so far, like the final experiment prototype! That was real nice last time I saw it, gave me a feeling despite being rough. I... I hope it still does.

[^1]: The desert is a planet in which the Shard resided for billions of years, assimilating the universe's rules and behaviors. Why a desert? Because, well, I feel as if sand is very related to light for reasons I don't exactly remember.
