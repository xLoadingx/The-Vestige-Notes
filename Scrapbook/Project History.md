# Original Concept and History

Start date: 2026-05-17

Well, originally this mod was going to be almost entirely different. I had thought of it after finishing the base game, simply because I had wanted more and I believed that the scout seen at the end of the game (if shot through the maelstrom or storm inside of the eye) was a very interesting open-ended question about the behavior of the Eye.

![[Scout in next universe.webp|797|477x298]]
> Apologies for the low-quality screenshot, it is *quite* difficult to find a good photo of the scout here!

I was like, wouldn't it be neat for there to be a continuation *after* the base game? Maybe a continuation into this new universe, or at least, a separate thing like the Echoes of the Eye DLC (which I hadn't finished at the time). I was waiting to finish that until I started so I didn't miss anything important.

But I felt just another DLC was pretty boring.

So with that, I was originally planning to make this mod all about the new universe. You would play as those little bug people and travel throughout the universe to find the remnants of the Hearthians in some way. The main mechanic behind this was the feeling of deja vu, since the player had beaten the game as the Hearthian before. It'd be similar to the way Slate is confused about your ramblings of time loops and death traps.

Another mechanic behind it was light in some shape or form since it connects to "information" and "time", which were the two words that were stuck in my head when thinking about it. I had thought of all of this before completing the DLC, so you could imagine my disappointment was immeasurable when I saw that it had already been done. I was worried it was just going to feel like the DLC all over again.

But then of course, I realized I'm one person who absolutely cannot build those huge planets and systems with new and unique rules. I cannot make Outer Wilds 2 lmao.

And as Riebeck (or, some form of them...) once said:
>"...The future is always built on the past, even if we won’t get to see it. Still, it’s um, time for something new, now."

---

So of course, I had to think of a new idea.

I still enjoyed the concept of light, so I just kind of... moved the story back a bit so you learn about the universe previous to the Hearthian one instead of making a whole new universe myself. That still exists in the mod today and is one of the core implications, but it's pretty different than what I had imagined back then.

At this point, I wasn't quite sure what to do with the mod. I had decided to cornily name it "The Vestige" at that point, since that was a word that described remnants of things that didn't exist anymore, and I thought it was a good placeholder name.

Clearly that name stuck around, but anyways.

I still hadn't beaten the DLC at this time, and I decided that I didn't want to touch on the Stranger or its inhabitants in this mod, though I still had to complete it so I didn't spoil myself looking through the game files.

I had described it as a fun side project, since the modding side of things was so much easier than my original modding community, RUMBLE VR. I figured this would be a great break from all the technical details and allow me to focus on the story more without worrying about implementation difficulty.

---

At this point, I decided to try and start looking through the lore of the game to see if I could find any connections that caught my Eye (heh), which is what is now called the Canon and Implications folder.

I knew the Eye had something more to offer, something more than just going so far to learn you can't go any further. Of course I never wanted to contradict this, but I felt there was a deeper layer. While I've yet to put it into words, I have a pretty good idea.

>"But writing this, this scientific but also symbolic extension of a game I adore, gives me a feeling that I didn't expect writing to ever give me.
> 
>Or, to put it simply, it's fun."

I've only been working on the mod for around 3 days, at least since starting the modding project on my computer. But I had already felt like I was going in the wrong direction. But I believed getting the early concept correct would be largely beneficial in the long run.

Specifically, I felt as if I was too focused on trying to make it fit into the game rather than exploring the areas it left untouched.

The Low-Light Observation Chamber, which indirectly came from the mechanic of light being used in some form, was made at this point, though its original purpose was not for the shard yet, and the Expansion was not thought of.

I had imagined it as some large building on Giant's Deep, due to the water and thick atmosphere blocking a ton of light from entering the building, which was indeed in its name. I still didn't know what it was for though.

---

I had finally completed the DLC and I was shocked at the reveal of the simulation and everything.

But I still thought I was going in the wrong direction. I had mentioned it to other people at this point and they seemed interested in what I could make knowing I have experience in modding, which was a nice motivation boost.

I was slightly jealous, to be honest, at how well the Outer Wilds developers are able to make such cool mechanics and tie everything up into one clean, though non-linear, narrative.

At this time, I started doing research into what other people had thought about the game to help, specifically [Scruffy Gushes about the ENDING of Outer Wilds](https://youtu.be/lheIxAMDr0U). Very nice video and greatly helped me think about the story.

I had also played other story mods to see what the bar was for "good" outer wilds story mods. This ended up not being a very healthy comparison. I wasn't *trying* to compare against other mods or downplay them in any way, but I simply tried to compare against things like Astral Codec too much and raised the bar far too high.

Now of course, I know the scope of this mod is insane, and I'm happy with long-term projects like that, and not everyone will like this mod. That helped put my mind at ease, though I still sometimes compare aspects to see what either of us did that I personally didn't enjoy. To be fair, I have a completely different idea about "story mod" in Outer Wilds than a lot of other people do. From what I've seen, a ton of people use the game simply as a base to drive their own sci-fi stories.

Anyways, I was attempting to make tools and other things to help me in my process of actually *making* the mod when I finished the story.

![[Nomai Text Tools.webp]]

I was again worrying at this point that it was too similar to how the game worked.

I had come up with the shard and the Residual Archive (which at the time I didn't know if I wanted, and honestly I still don't), and I felt the progression with the Shard -> LOC -> Residual archive was too similar to the Clue -> HEL -> ATP progression, they were practically the same. You find a clue that lets you get into the locked building, do an experiment inside of it, and then that leads you to a larger scale version of that experiment.

I had decided at this point that there were two halves to the story:
- A temporal side, with the Negative Time Interval and the High-Energy Lab
- An informational side, with the Information Control learned from the shard in the Low-Light Observation Chamber

I was pretty happy with how it was so far, and I eventually realized that the story being too similar to the base game is simply how trying to implement an extension of an existing story works. It used the same mechanics, visual language, etc. so a lot of the things are going to be similar.

And also, I'm the developer, I see a ton of things players don't (well, unless they read this of course), so I can easily overlook things or expect things from players that only make sense to me within the context of the mod (specifically things like solving "puzzles")

And thus it snowballed from there into where the story is today (8/9/2026), with the LOC being the spine of the entire mod, and everything branching out from that!

Of course there's other things I didn't mention here, but this is basically where the core of the story came from and the rest are simply things I've already written (for the most part) inside of the other notes, along with the scrapped designs written amongst the other notes in this folder..
