As described in [[Doubt (8-23-2026)]], I had thought of this as a way to bring the player towards the previous universe in an abstract sort of way.

You would go through the Eye like normal, and due to your recent discoveries with the Shard and the Nomai, you would instead find yourself in front of a massive "[[Fabric of Spacetime.webp|connection]]" while the other, smaller ones swirled around it.

Going through this would lead you to the desert scene (which in of itself is not scrapped). Now, I really did believe that this was a great idea, so I decided to build it, unlike most things I've came up with so far.

![[Scrapped - Previous Universe Wormhole.webp|420]]

It... didn't end up great? Most likely due to both the lack of meaning and a lack of technical ability. I haven't actually developed most of the story yet, so, building such a meaningful tieback to everything without everything actually existing just didn't make it mean much.

So, I scrapped it, after feeling a lot of doubt about the whole thing.

Could it have been cool? Absolutely, but, it's just not that time yet.
