When I was originally writing the "Spacetime" note, I had the idea of visualizing the spacetime medium during the Memory Flashback sequence after death.

I had actually gone as far as creating the whole thing since I thought it'd be such a cool idea.

Basically it was when you died, instead of going through the whole mask and seeing your memories thing, you simply woke up in a black void without your spacesuit, and everything was muffled.

Then soon there'd be the text you normally see flying by in like an area around a floor, where there'd be a little visual representation of how you died, along with the mask in it, and if you interacted with the mask, it'd bring you to the original sequence.

The idea behind this was that you'd have to try and figure out what this place was and why you were suddenly being shown it.

The main problems I had was why this suddenly happens despite it not happening in the other (unmodded) loops, and what it actually meant. It was a cool idea, but sadly it didn't feel as cool as I thought when I made it, so I scrapped it for what we have currently.

While I still don't know exactly what I'll use the wormhole connection found in the quantum fabric for, I feel it'll be similar.
