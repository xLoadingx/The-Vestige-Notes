One of the earliest endings I can remember coming up with for The Vestige was something with the Residual Archive. It was a poor name choice, and I was planning to rename it (before I later scrapped the idea altogether to merge it with the Expansion).

The Residual Archive was meant to be an even larger building in comparison to the Low-Light Observation chamber, as part of the Shard Research Group's main location of knowledge and study, similar to the Tower of Quantum Knowledge. However, as the LOC eventually grew to reach the size of the entire inside of Ember Twin, I knew that this Residual Archive had lost its power in the story.

Along with that, it also was the main ending for the story. It's merged version still is as of now, the 6th room in the Expansion, but this one was different.

As far as I can remember, I had considered the idea that not only did quantum objects allow you to entangle through space, but also through time. Thinking back on it now, perhaps quantum objects moved positions not because they exist in all places at once, but because they were simply switching timelines in which they had been in different places.

But anyways, my idea was that the Residual Archive was only accessible through this time-entanglement. I had a few issues with this however. I didn't know why it was only accessible in the future / past. 

Maybe it had broken down and was inaccessible all this time later? Boring, and it provided no puzzle for the player except for finding the quantum shard that accomplished this. Maybe it was only accessible in the future? That didn't even make sense, how would the Nomai make something that only worked long after they were gone? What would be the point?

And also, why do it altogether? The Nomai would want the research to be accessible to anyone, and this seems a bit more complicated than necessary.

So, in the end I scrapped it, because it provided unnecessary difficulty, made no sense, and didn't actually give a puzzle to the player.
