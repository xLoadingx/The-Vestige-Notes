#Phenomenon #Major #Theme
The [[Persistence#Law of Persistence|Law of Persistence]] is based off the fact that the hatchling's scout can be found in the next universal cycle after the hatchling enters the Eye (though only if the scout was shot into the [[Quantum Behavior|quantum]] storm or maelstrom found within).

The other laws mentioned below are based off the fact that the scout seemingly behaves normally (as it would in the Hearthian Universe) despite the changes in the way gravity and the sorts behaves, as seen in the new planet formations.

---

# Law of Persistence
The Law of Persistence is a broad term that encompasses both the [[Persistence#Law of Retention|Law of Retention]] and the [[Persistence#Law of Assimilation|Law of Assimilation]].

The Law of Persistence applies when an object happens to go into the Eye before a conscious observer enters.

Unlike all [[Spacetime#Matter|Matter]] and [[Spacetime#Information|Information]] getting destroyed and their rules rewritten after a conscious observer collapses the Probability Sphere, an object entering the Eye itself skips this process and proceeds directly into the next universal cycle. 

Though the means in which the object survives the creation of the universe is unknown, it is able to exist from one universe to the next through the Eye.

## Law of Retention
When an object persists into the next universal cycle, it skips the process in which the Eye rewrites the [[Spacetime#Matter|Matter's]] rules[^1]. This allows it to keep it's properties from it's origin universe while persisting into the next.

As seen with [[The Shard]], the interaction between two different rulesets can make odd interactions occur.

## Law of Assimilation
As a kind of counterforce to the [[Persistence#Law of Retention|Law of Retention]], the Law of Assimilation allows objects to slowly obtain the rules of the universe it is *currently* located in. However, this only occurs over an immense span of time.

# Quantum Objects (A Special Case)
[[Quantum Behavior|Quantum Objects]] are a unique case in terms of applying the [[Persistence#Law of Persistence|Law of Persistence]].

Quantum objects exhibit [[Spacetime#^e978e3|information-based behavior]], one of the only objects that do it naturally across all universal cycles. Because of this, the [[Persistence#Law of Assimilation|Law of Assimilation]] applies much faster (though still slowly) to quantum objects due to their more malleable form, both informationally and materialistically.

Macroscale quantum objects also seem to exhibit their own form of "pocket-universe" that has the rules of the Eye (ex. the Quantum Moon, Quantum Shards[^2], the Eye itself), which has separate assimilable rules from the current universal cycle. Thus why quantum behavior seems to spread outwards from quantum objects, as objects nearby assimilate into this new ruleset.

And because the Eye does not get destroyed between universal cycles, thus applying the [[Persistence#Law of Persistence|Law of Persistence]] to itself, it skips the rule-change process and retains it's "local rules" it had from the start. This is why quantum shards do not change behavior between universal cycles, except for special cases such as [[The Shard]].

The length of time that most quantum shards have been exposed to the local ruleset of the Eye causes their behavior to be deeply ingrained, so much so that their local rules are hardly ever overwritten and only added onto.

[^1]: I'm not actually sure what the "rules" are in the actual physical sense. Perhaps it is a more fundamental version of [[Spacetime#Information|Information]] that the Eye manages to encode.

[^2]: To much less of a degree, quantum shards exhibit this same "pocket-universe" behavior, though it is too small for the [[Persistence#Law of Assimilation|Law of Assimilation]] not to apply.
