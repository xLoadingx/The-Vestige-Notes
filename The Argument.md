#Theme
The Argument is a series of conversations throughout many locations in the mod that occur because of the disagreement on how to prioritize the two tenets of Nomai philosophy in the search for the Eye of the Universe:

> SOLANUM: These are the two tenets of Nomai philosophy; to seek out and to understand is our way of living.


But how can one seek out without understanding? How can one understand without seeking out? 

This is the problem that splits the Nomai when the idea of the [[Nomai Technology#The Ash Twin Project|Ash Twin Project]] is proposed.
