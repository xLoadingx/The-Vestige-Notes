#Phenomenon #Mechanic

Data Streams are what I like to call Information traveling through [[Spacetime]]. While canonically they are not actual flying streams of Nomai Text, it's the way the game already represents it as found within [[Nomai Technology#The Ash Twin Project|the Ash Twin Project]] near the end of the loop.

It is also used as a natural curiosity, as it can be seen at the start of the loop and near the end of the loop, especially around the system of the [[Nomai Technology#Orbital Probe Cannon|Orbital Probe Cannon]].

![[ATP to OPC Data Stream.mp4]]
> Ended up really liking this effect, though I had to delay the firing of the cannon so it could be slow enough to be visible.

What I particularly like about this is the fact that it adds an immediate separate path from the Museum Placard, which is one of the issues I've been recently struggling with as of 9/7/2026. The player can either follow the title screen hint, or they could get up and go to the Orbital Probe Cannon in order to see what the bright streak was.

---

This is also visible inside of the original [[Low-Light Observation Chamber]], when activating the experiment. These information streams get detached from [[The Shard]] as the light passes through it and onto the photosensitive paper.

It is mostly used as a visual hint towards the specific phenomenon of pure information traveling through spacetime.
