#Theme 

Currently I have two ideas for the ending to The Vestige.

#### Archaeological Ending

One of the first things you encounter in the mod is a dead Nomai with the fragment. This is only referenced to later here in the ending, located inside of the [[Low-Light Observation Chamber#Expansion|Expansion's]] 6th hallway.

The "understand" side of the Nomai [[The Argument|argument]] is located here, as the other 5 were used (and built after the Final Experiment) for ATP research on each location. While the High-Energy Lab says it's being used to design the Ash Twin Project, I do believe they only meant the warp towers, as that's all that can be found inside.

The LOC's 5 hallways (or more accurately 'corners') can be used for these since there are 5 locations in which is needed to construct the ATP:
1. Timber Hearth
2. Brittle Hollow
3. Giant's Deep
4. Sun Station
5. Hourglass Twins

However, the LOC is a 6 sided structure, so they decided to allow the "understand" group to have their own little area of research. They used this area to try and further understand [[The Shard]]. They got the closest they ever had to figuring out where it came from, along with some of the behaviors of the Eye.

However, of course, the Interloper killed them before they could.

The reason this area is so significant[^2] is because it's where a ton of the Nomai collected after the Sun Station failed, desperately searching for any other way to find the Eye.[^3]

Music plays quite a big role in this as it's quite mundane otherwise, and there has to be a reason for the player to care (which is partly why it references the first thing you find). In order for them to care, they have to have at least some idea of what happened.

Besides the reference to the dead Nomai, the argument is a huge part in what makes it emotional. The debate between the two tenets of Nomai Philosophy.

>SOLANUM: These are the two tenets of Nomai philosophy; to seek out and to understand is our way of living.

As mentioned, the "understanding" side are the group of Nomai who wanted to know what they were working with before extrapolating the knowledge they gained into such a giant and dangerous project such as the ATP.

Ultimately, at least culturally and mechanically, the "seek out" side won out, continuing with building the ATP up until the Sun Station failed.

The debate of these two groups go on throughout the mod and is one of the core metaphors, and this 6th room shows the climax of that. Not climax as in, like, they get super angry at each other, but instead the opposite. The argument falters, in some shape or form, I'm not sure.

It's less something specific and more just a culmination of what the player learned throughout the mod into a single room of saying that they failed. And music, of course. I've learned it's like 50% of the emotion, personally.
