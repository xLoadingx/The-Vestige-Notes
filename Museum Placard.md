#Location #Detail
A new placard found in the Museum on Timber Hearth can be found that starts the hatchling off on in the story. It states that a variation of the quantum signal found on the Grove shard and other quantum shards in the solar system can be heard coming from Brittle Hollow. However, only Riebeck has travelled to Brittle Hollow[^1]. ^tr-pktan3oj1

This variation of course is referencing to a fragment of [[The Shard]].

> *These strange rocks found throughout the solar system all give off the same distinctive signal.

> Because this signal remains recognizable even when the rocks appear to move, it can still be used to locate them again.
> 
> *Small differences have been recorded between individual rocks, though this effect is usually quite tiny.

> If you'd like to hear them for yourself, please ask Hornfels for a temporary signalscope![^2]

[^1]: While Riebeck has heard of the variation shard since he was a kid, he wanted to get on with studying the Nomai cities below. He mentions this in dialogue when asked.

[^2]: You can ask Hornfels for a signalscope back near the statue, though they will tell you that you already have one in dialogue
